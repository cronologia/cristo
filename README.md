# Cristo — Cronologia

A source-referenced chronology of Jesus Christ, of the objects venerated as his
relics, and of where those objects are kept today. Part of the
[Cronologia](https://cronologia.github.io/) family (en / es / pt).

**Status: bootstrap in progress.** The site is not built yet. `research/` holds
the verified research of record, committed first so it cannot be lost:

- `research/strand-b-cloth-image-blood-relics.md` — the Shroud of Turin, the
  Sudarium of Oviedo, the Veronica and Manoppello, the Holy Tunic of Trier and
  of Argenteuil, the Mandylion, the Holy Blood, the Holy Prepuce.
- `research/strand-c-instruments-of-the-passion.md` — the True Cross, the
  Titulus, the Nails, the Crown of Thorns, the Holy Lances, the Scala Sancta,
  the Columns of the Flagellation, the Holy Chalice, the Holy Crib.

Sourcing posture (core ADR-0007, applied to relics): a record states one
authority's act about one named object, citing the document. Expositions,
veneration and shrines are acts of cult, not findings of authenticity;
laboratory results are the laboratory's, and published critiques are recorded
as critiques.
