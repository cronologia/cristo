# cronologia/cristo: relic visitor details (location, coordinates, object, visibility, status)

Research only; no repo files touched. Fetches on 2026-09-25 through the session proxy (curl, desktop-browser UA). "Fetched" = HTTP 200 and the quoted text was read in the returned page. Facts already quoted with a URL in `research/strand-b-cloth-image-blood-relics.md` (strand B) or `strand-c-instruments-of-the-passion.md` (strand C), fetched 2026-09-24, are reused and marked "(strand B/C)".

**Geocoding method.** OpenStreetMap Nominatim, `https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=<query>&format=json&limit=3`, one request every 1.2–3 s, descriptive User-Agent. Every chosen hit was checked by its `display_name` (right building, right city). Coordinates are OSM's point for the building (usually the centroid of its outline), not the chapel inside it. Two queries returned HTTP 429 (rate limit) and were re-run after a pause. Note: the very first query (Turin) carried a contact e-mail in the User-Agent; it was removed from every later request.

**House rules applied.** Attribution, never an assertion of authenticity. Acts of cult (expositions, feasts, papal visits, indulgences) are not authenticity findings (ADR-0007). "(not found)" = not located in a fetched source, not a claim that it does not exist.

## 1. shroud-turin — Shroud of Turin (Sindone)

- **Where:** Cathedral of San Giovanni Battista (Duomo di Torino), piazza San Giovanni, Turin, Italy. Custodian: "È custodita nella Cattedrale di Torino (piazza San Giovanni), nell'ultima cappella della navata sinistra, sotto la Tribuna Reale. Per visitare la cappella non occorre prenotazione." ("It is kept in Turin Cathedral (piazza San Giovanni), in the last chapel of the left nave, under the Royal Tribune. No booking is needed to visit the chapel.") [sindone-dove, https://sindone.org/la-sindone/dove-la-sindone/, dateModified 2024-04-16, fetched 2026-09-25]
- **Coordinates:** 45.0733274, 7.6854108. Query "Cattedrale di San Giovanni Battista, Torino" → 1 hit, chosen: "Cattedrale di San Giovanni Battista, Piazza San Giovanni, Quadrilatero Romano, Centro, Circoscrizione 1, Torino, Piemonte, 10122, Italia" (amenity/place_of_worship). No rejected hits.
- **Object:** "un lenzuolo di lino tessuto a spina di pesce delle dimensioni di circa m. 4,41 x 1,13, contenente la doppia immagine accostata per il capo del cadavere di un uomo" ("a herringbone-woven linen sheet of about 4.41 × 1.13 m, bearing the double image, head to head, of the body of a man"); framed by "due linee nere strinate e da una serie di lacune" from the Chambéry fire of 1532 [sindone-cose, https://sindone.org/la-sindone/cose-la-sindone/, dateModified 2021-09-16].
- **Case:** "Il Telo si trova dentro la «teca per la conservazione», che a sua volta è rinchiusa in una grande cassa metallica." ("The cloth is inside the 'conservation case', which is itself enclosed in a large metal chest.") [sindone-dove]
- **Can it be seen:** No, except at public expositions. "La Sindone non si può vedere. … Normalmente viene estratta solo in occasione delle ostensioni pubbliche." Pilgrims can pray outside the chapel, "proprio di fronte alla cassa che racchiude la Sindone" (in front of the chest). Cathedral open daily 9–12.30 and 15–19; chapel not visitable during Masses. Next exposition: "nessuno sa quando sarà la prossima ostensione" [sindone-dove, page dated 2024-04-16].
- **Status:** First attestation: Lirey, 1350s (CE "Troyes": exposed at Lirey collegiate church founded 20 Jun 1353; CE "Holy Shroud": "about the year 1360") (strand B). Dating: Damon et al., *Nature* 337:611–615 (16 Feb 1989), "AD 1260-1390, with at least 95% confidence" (Arizona, Oxford, Zurich); contested by Casabianca et al., *Archaeometry* 2019 (strand B). Church position: custodian's site says the tradition "non può ancora dirsi definitivamente provata" [sindone-cose]; John Paul II, 24 May 1998: "the Church has no specific competence to pronounce on these questions" (strand B). No authenticity act located.
- **Gaps:** none of the required fields missing.
